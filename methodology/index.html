Methodology

Trust is the product.

ShipRank exists because giant directories will list anything. This site will not put a number on a tool we have not run, and it will not let anyone pay to look better.

What a ranking is

A ranking is one outcome a solo builder is trying to finish. The current jobs areGet a landing page live, Build an app with no code, Automate a business workflow, Make short-form video content, Build an AI agent. A tool gets on a page only when the vendor’s own site says the product can do that job. That “why” line is a citation, not a review.

A full test sorts by composite. Partial tests sit under those rows with no rank number. Untested tools stay A–Z under that. Pending is an empty test, not a zero and not a tie.

How a score will be calculated

Each ranking publishes criteria. Weights on a page add up to 100.

  1. Score every criterion from 0 to 10 against a written task.
  2. Multiply each score by its weight.
  3. Add those products and divide by 10. That is the composite, from 0 to 100.
  4. Sort fully tested tools by that composite. Show one decimal when the result is not a whole number. Do not round 76.5 up to 77.
  5. Leave partial runs unranked. Record the criteria that were actually measured and mark the rest as not measured.
  6. Keep untested tools in an A–Z block. They cannot sort above a tested tool.

A composite is allowed only for rankingTests[rankingId].status: "tested", with a version, a date, evidence notes, a methodology note, and a 0–10 on every criterion. The stored composite must equal the weighted math.partial keeps the composite null. The validator rejects a rank score on a partial test.

The first published run is “Get a landing page live,” free plans, 8–9 Nov 2026, Ember & Oak prompt, then the headline and orange signup-button edit. Bolt is 76.5. Lovable is 59.5. v0 is partial.

What the evidence note has to contain

  • The version or plan we actually used, and the date.
  • The task, in enough detail that someone else could rerun it.
  • What happened, including the failure.
  • The criterion scores, not just the composite.

A vendor’s benchmark, a tweet, or a sponsored demo is not evidence.

Disclosure

  • Rankings are not for sale. A company cannot buy inclusion, a higher score, a badge, or a better sort position.
  • Sponsorship, if it ever exists, is labeled and kept off the ranking pages’ order.
  • Affiliate links must set affiliate: true. The site then marks the card, the row, and the outbound link. This seed has zero affiliate links.
  • Prices are copied from the vendor page named in the record, with a last-checked date. If a toggle (monthly vs annual) was ambiguous, the record says so instead of picking a number.
  • We do not invent launch dates. releaseDate stays null until an official source dates it.

Who this is for

Vibe coders and people building a business by themselves. The same readers often care about agents, security, games, and funding. Those interests do not get a looser standard. A security tool or a funding tool lands here only when we can score it the same way, against a job, with evidence. None are in this seed, because we are not going to pad the directory to look broader than the tests.
