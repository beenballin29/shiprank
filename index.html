Method

How a number gets on this site

Pick the job

A ranking is a finished outcome, like a live URL or a clip you can post. Not a category with 400 logos.

Run it, write it down

Version, date, what we asked, what happened, and a 0–10 on each criterion. No note, no score.

Publish the math

Weights are on the page. The composite is the weighted average, scaled to 100. Sort follows the score, never a fee.
