Grok

xAI’s assistant. Consumer access is The X Subscription, shared with Cursor.

Official siteCompareNo affiliateuntested

Grok is xAI’s assistant for chat, voice, images, video, and agents. New self-serve access is The X Subscription, the same plans that include Cursor. Grok Bot, the agent, starts on Premium. Billing for the subscription is managed at grok.com.

Features and prices below are from the vendor site on the last-checked date. They are not ShipRank test results.

What the vendor lists

  • Pricing page lists chat, voice, image and video generation (Imagine), and Build.
  • Grok Bot is included on Premium, Super, and Ultra, and is not included on Plus, per the xAI pricing doc.
  • The included usage pool is shared with Cursor and refreshes weekly.
  • Super’s plan card lists 1080p videos up to 15 seconds.

Use cases

Draft and research · Generate short video · Run a multi-step agent

Pricing · checked 2026-11-08

The X Subscription list prices on x.ai/pricing and the xAI docs: Free $0, Plus $8/month, Premium $30/month, Super $100/month, Ultra $200/month. Annual prices in the docs are $80, $300, $1,000, and $2,000. Docs last-updated line on the pricing article was October 11, 2026. Prices exclude tax.

Free tier: yes

Free includes limited chat and limited agent requests, per the pricing page.

  • Free — $0 · Limited chat and agent requests.
  • Plus — $8/month · Annual $80. Chat, voice, Imagine, Build, API, and Cursor. No Grok Bot.
  • Premium — $30/month · Annual $300. 4x Plus usage. Includes Grok Bot.
  • Super — $100/month · Annual $1,000. 15x Plus usage. Page lists 1080p, 15-second videos.
  • Ultra — $200/month · Annual $2,000. 50x Plus usage.
