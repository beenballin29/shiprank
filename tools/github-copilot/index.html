GitHub Copilot

GitHub’s coding assistant, billed with a seat fee plus AI credits.

Official siteCompareNo affiliateuntested

GitHub Copilot is a coding assistant in the editor, CLI, and Copilot app. Current plan docs price individuals at Free, Pro, Pro+, and Max, and organizations at Business and Enterprise. Usage past the included credits is the metered part. This record uses the plan docs, not the older premium-request system.

Features and prices below are from the vendor site on the last-checked date. They are not ShipRank test results.

What the vendor lists

  • Plan docs say every plan includes the Copilot CLI and the Copilot app.
  • Free is limited to automatic model selection. Paid plans add a monthly AI-credit allowance.
  • Business and Enterprise add organization policy controls. Enterprise adds a larger credit pool.
  • A separate “Copilot access” enterprise model has no included allowance. It is not the default self-serve plan.

Pricing · checked 2026-11-08

From GitHub’s Copilot plan docs: Free $0, Pro $10/month, Pro+ $39/month, Max $100/month, Business $19 per seat/month, Enterprise $39 per seat/month. The same docs list individual totals of 1,500, 7,000, and 20,000 AI credits a month on Pro, Pro+, and Max, each split into a base amount and a flex allotment.

Free tier: yes

Copilot Free includes a limited AI-credit allowance and auto model selection only. Verified students can get Copilot Student, also free.

  • Free — $0 · Limited credits. Auto model selection only.
  • Pro — $10/month · 1,000 base credits plus 500 flex, 1,500 total.
  • Pro+ — $39/month · 3,900 base plus 3,100 flex, 7,000 total. Premium models.
  • Max — $100/month · 10,000 base plus 10,000 flex, 20,000 total.
  • Business — $19 per seat/month · 1,900 credits per user/month, pooled.
  • Enterprise — $39 per seat/month · 3,900 credits per user/month, pooled.
