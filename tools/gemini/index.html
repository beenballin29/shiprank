Gemini

Google’s assistant app, with paid Google AI plans and a coding-agent product called Antigravity.

Official siteCompareNo affiliateuntested

Gemini is Google’s consumer AI app. Paid access is sold as Google AI Plus, Pro, and Ultra through Google One. The subscriptions page also describes Google Antigravity as agents that write and deploy code, with a free starting point. Prices below are the US figures on the Google One AI plans page.

Features and prices below are from the vendor site on the last-checked date. They are not ShipRank test results.

What the vendor lists

  • Deep Research and image generation are listed on the free Gemini plan.
  • Paid plans add higher limits, Gemini inside Google apps, and monthly Google Flow credits for video.
  • The subscriptions page describes Antigravity as autonomous coding agents, with a no-cost start and higher limits on paid plans.
  • Pro is the first personal plan on the Google One table that includes Gemini 4 Argon.

Use cases

Draft and research · Code with an agent · Generate video

Pricing · checked 2026-11-08

From Google One’s AI plans page: Plus $4.99/month, Pro $19.99/month, Ultra 5x $99.99/month, Ultra 20x $199.99/month. The Gemini app also has a free plan with a Google account. The subscriptions page lists business plans starting at $21 per seat/month.

Free tier: yes

Free, with a Google account, includes the Gemini app on a Flash-Lite model, image generation, Deep Research, and 15 GB of storage shared with Gmail, Drive, and Photos.

  • Free — $0 · Google account. 15 GB storage.
  • Google AI Plus — $4.99/month · 400 GB storage. 200 Flow credits/month.
  • Google AI Pro — $19.99/month · 5 TB storage. Gemini 4 Argon. 1,000 Flow credits/month.
  • Google AI Ultra 5x — $99.99/month · 5x Pro usage limits. 20 TB storage. 10,000 Flow credits.
  • Google AI Ultra 20x — $199.99/month · 20x Pro usage limits. 30 TB storage. 25,000 Flow credits.
