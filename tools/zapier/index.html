Zapier

App automation billed in tasks, with agents listed on the same pricing page.

Official siteCompareNo affiliateuntested

Zapier connects apps in workflows called Zaps and also lists Agents, chatbots, and MCP on its pricing page. Paid plans pick a task tier. The table below is the entry of that table, not every tier. Higher volume costs more, and the full grid is on zapier.com/pricing.

Features and prices below are from the vendor site on the last-checked date. They are not ShipRank test results.

What the vendor lists

  • A task is the usage meter for Zaps. Free stops at two-step workflows.
  • Paid plans add multi-step Zaps, filters, paths, and webhooks.
  • The pricing page lists Agents, chatbots, and MCP, with a free allowance and higher limits on paid plans.
  • Code steps can run JavaScript or Python, with a short included runtime that is longer on paid plans.

Use cases

Automate a repeated task · Run a multi-step agent

Pricing · checked 2026-11-08

Free is 100 tasks/month. Professional at 750 tasks is $19.99/month on annual billing or $29.99/month on monthly billing. Team starts at 2,000 tasks: $69/month annual or $103.50/month monthly, for up to 25 users. The page’s price table continues up to 2,000,000 tasks. Enterprise is sales. Annual is described as 33% off monthly.

Free tier: yes

Free includes 100 tasks/month, unlimited two-step Zaps, and basic access to Agents, chatbots, and MCP. Multi-step Zaps require a paid plan.

  • Free — $0 · 100 tasks/month. Two-step Zaps only.
  • Professional — From $19.99/month annual · 750-task tier. Monthly billing at that tier is $29.99. Multi-step Zaps, webhooks, premium apps.
  • Team — From $69/month annual · 2,000-task tier. Monthly billing is $103.50. Up to 25 users. SSO.
  • Enterprise — Contact sales · Unlimited users. Annual task limit instead of a monthly reset.
