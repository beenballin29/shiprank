Bolt

Browser app builder from StackBlitz, with hosting on paid and free plans.

Official siteCompareNo affiliatetested

Bolt is StackBlitz’s AI builder for sites and apps. It runs in the browser. The pricing page lists a free plan with hosting and daily token limits, Pro at $25/month, and Teams at $30 per member/month. A separate Bolt Lite page advertised a $9/month plan and said sign-ups close October 14, without a year, so that offer is not treated as a current plan here.

Features and prices below are from the vendor site on the last-checked date. They are not ShipRank test results.

What the vendor lists

  • Builds websites and apps in the browser. Bolt’s FAQ says the product runs on StackBlitz WebContainers, so there is no local install.
  • Free hosting on a bolt.host URL, with a request cap listed on the pricing page.
  • Pro adds a custom domain, removes Bolt branding, and lets unused monthly tokens roll over.
  • Token limits are the meter: daily and monthly on Free, monthly starting at 10M on Pro.

Use cases

Publish a landing page · Build an app in the browser

Pricing · checked 2026-11-08

Free is $0, with 300K tokens a day, 1M tokens a month, and Bolt branding on sites. Pro is $25/month, with no daily token limit and a monthly allowance that starts at 10M tokens. Teams is $30 per member/month. Enterprise is custom. The page says annual billing saves up to 28%.

Free tier: yes

Free includes public and private projects, website hosting, and a bolt.host URL. Custom domains and removing Bolt branding require a paid plan.

  • Free — $0 · 300K tokens/day, 1M tokens/month, hosting, Bolt branding.
  • Pro — $25/month · Starts at 10M tokens/month. Custom domain. Unused tokens roll over.
  • Teams — $30 per member/month · Shared billing and admin controls.
  • Enterprise — Custom · SSO, audit logs, dedicated support.
