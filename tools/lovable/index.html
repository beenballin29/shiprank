Lovable

Browser app builder billed in credits, with a free daily build allowance.

Official siteCompareNo affiliatetested

Lovable builds and publishes apps in the browser. Paid plans are sized by monthly credits, not by seats. The figures below come from Lovable’s subscription-plan docs and the June 13, 2026 billing post. The free plan can publish.

Features and prices below are from the vendor site on the last-checked date. They are not ShipRank test results.

What the vendor lists

  • Build and publish from the browser. The FAQ says the free plan can publish.
  • One credit balance covers building, chat, hosting and backend, and AI features in the published app.
  • Pro is where Lovable’s FAQ places custom domains and code editing.
  • A June 13, 2026 post said plan prices were not changing when Cloud and AI balances moved into the same credit.

Use cases

Build an app in the browser · Publish a landing page

Pricing · checked 2026-11-08

Free is $0. Pro starts at $25/month for 100 credits, or $250/year ($21/month). Business starts at $50/month for 100 credits, or $500/year. Both have higher credit tiers up to 10,000 credits a month. Enterprise is a custom contract. Daily build credits are separate from the monthly bundle.

Free tier: yes

Free includes 5 build credits a day, capped at 30 a month, plus 20 Cloud credits a month and 4 credits for AI features inside apps. The FAQ says you can build and publish without paying.

  • Free — $0 · 5 daily build credits, max 30/month.
  • Pro — From $25/month · 100 credits at $25/month or $250/year. Tiers up to 10,000 credits.
  • Business — From $50/month · 100 credits at $50/month or $500/year. Adds governance controls.
  • Enterprise — Custom
