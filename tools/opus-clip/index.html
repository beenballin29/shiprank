Opus Clip

Cuts long videos into short clips with captions and reframing.

Official siteCompareNo affiliateuntested

OpusClip turns a longer video into short clips. The pricing page lists Free, Starter, Pro, and Business. The page header says OpusClip 4.0 is here, without a date, so no release date is recorded for that line.

Features and prices below are from the vendor site on the last-checked date. They are not ShipRank test results.

What the vendor lists

  • AI clipping with control over clip length, from under a minute up to 15 minutes, on every plan.
  • Captions, emoji highlights, and 9:16 reframing are listed from the free plan, with a watermark on free caption templates.
  • Pro adds a scheduler, bulk export, and more than one aspect ratio.
  • Pro includes a team workspace for 2 users, up to 4.

Use cases

Cut clips from a longer recording · Caption a short video

Pricing · checked 2026-11-08

The pricing table lists Free at $0, Starter at $15/month, Pro at $29/month, and Business as custom. Credit amounts for each paid tier were not in the extracted table, so they are not stated.

Free tier: yes

Free can create clips. Caption templates are watermarked, exports expire after 3 days, and there is no watermark-free export on Free.

  • Free — $0 · Watermarked captions. Exports expire after 3 days.
  • Starter — $15/month · Watermark-free export. Posts to social. Storage expires after 29 days.
  • Pro — $29/month · Scheduler, 100 GB storage, team workspace, more aspect ratios.
  • Business — Custom · SSO, unlimited seats, dedicated queue.
