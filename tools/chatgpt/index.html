OpenAI’s chat product, with a free plan and a $20 Plus plan.

ChatGPT is OpenAI’s consumer app for chat, files, images, and coding tools. The pricing page names Free, Go, Plus, Pro, Business, and Enterprise. Dollar amounts for Go and the three Pro tiers were not in the text extracted from that page on the check date, so they are omitted. The Plus help article states Plus is $20/month.

Features and prices below are from the vendor site on the last-checked date. They are not ShipRank test results.

What the vendor lists

  • Vendor lists, on the Plus page: advanced reasoning models, image creation, deep research, projects, custom GPTs, Codex, and ChatGPT Work.
  • Vendor lists, on the Pro page: Dot, described there as an always-on agent, plus longer Codex sessions.
  • Plus help article lists voice, image generation, file uploads, and deep research as expanded features.
  • The Plus help article says API usage is not included.

Use cases

Draft and research · Code with an agent · Generate images

Pricing · checked 2026-11-08

A free plan exists. Plus is $20/month, billed monthly, per OpenAI’s help center, and API usage is billed separately. The pricing page also lists Go, Pro with three usage tiers, Business, and Enterprise. Those other dollar prices were not in the extracted page text on 2026-11-08.
