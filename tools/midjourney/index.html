Midjourney

Image generation, with video minutes included on paid plans.

Official siteCompareNo affiliateuntested

Midjourney generates images and, on current plans, video. There is no free tier on the official plan comparison. Commercial use is allowed under Midjourney’s terms, with a revenue rule for larger companies. Prices below are monthly and annual figures from that comparison article.

Features and prices below are from the vendor site on the last-checked date. They are not ShipRank test results.

What the vendor lists

  • Image jobs and video generation are both on the plan comparison. Video resolution depends on the plan.
  • Relax mode, which queues jobs instead of spending Fast hours, starts on Standard for images.
  • Stealth mode, which keeps generations private, is Pro and Mega only.
  • The same article says a company with over $1,000,000 USD gross revenue a year must use Pro or Mega under the commercial terms.

Use cases

Generate images · Generate a short video clip

Pricing · checked 2026-11-08

Monthly prices: Basic $10, Standard $30, Pro $60, Mega $120. Annual billing is 20% off: $96, $288, $576, and $1,152 per year. Extra Fast GPU time is $4/hour on every plan. No free plan is listed.

Free tier: no

The plan comparison does not list a free tier or a free trial on the website. Basic is the entry plan.

  • Basic — $10/month · Annual $96. About 3.3 Fast hours. SD video. No Relax mode.
  • Standard — $30/month · Annual $288. 15 Fast hours. Unlimited Relax images. SD and HD video.
  • Pro — $60/month · Annual $576. 30 Fast hours. Stealth mode. Relax image and SD video.
  • Mega — $120/month · Annual $1,152. 60 Fast hours. Stealth mode.
