Gamma

Generates decks, docs, and simple websites from a prompt.

Official siteCompareNo affiliateuntested

Gamma generates presentations, documents, websites, and images. The pricing page shows annual per-seat prices and says annual billing saves up to 28% versus monthly. Free credits do not refill.

Features and prices below are from the vendor site on the last-checked date. They are not ShipRank test results.

What the vendor lists

  • Vendor lists presentations, docs, websites, social posts, and images on the free plan.
  • Plus removes Gamma branding and raises the per-prompt slide cap to 100.
  • Pro adds custom branding, analytics, and custom-domain publishing.
  • Credits are the meter for AI actions. Paid unused credits roll over up to 2x the monthly amount, per the FAQ.

Use cases

Publish a landing page · Make a deck

Pricing · checked 2026-11-08

Prices shown as annual billing: Plus $9 per seat/month ($108/year), Pro $18 per seat/month ($216/year), Ultra $90 per seat/month ($1,080/year). Free is $0. The page says this annual price is up to 28% less than monthly. Monthly dollar amounts are not repeated here because they were not the figures next to the plan cards in the extract.

Free tier: yes

Free includes 400 credits at signup. The pricing FAQ says those credits do not refill. You can earn more by referring people, up to a balance cap.

  • Free — $0 · 400 starter credits. Up to 10 slides per prompt.
  • Plus — $9/seat/month, billed annually · $108/year. 1,500 credits/month. Removes Gamma branding.
  • Pro — $18/seat/month, billed annually · $216/year. 4,000 credits/month. Up to 10 custom domains.
  • Ultra — $90/seat/month, billed annually · $1,080/year. 20,000 credits/month. Up to 100 custom domains.
