Pricing · checked 2026-11-08

Free is $0. The page showed Hobbyist at $16, Creator at $24, and Business at $50 per person per month, beside copy that annual billing saves up to 35% versus monthly. Confirm the toggle on descript.com/pricing before you treat those as the monthly rate.
