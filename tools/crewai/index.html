CrewAI’s pricing page sells a build-and-run platform for agent workflows. The free plan includes a visual editor and 50 workflow executions a month. Enterprise is custom and adds governance and deployment options. This record is about that hosted platform, not a separate package license.

Features and prices below are from the vendor site on the last-checked date. They are not ShipRank test results.

What the vendor lists

  • Visual editor and AI copilot on the free plan.
  • GitHub integration and workflow templates are listed on the plan comparison.
  • Free plan is capped at 50 executions a month on the pricing page.
  • Enterprise adds SSO, RBAC, and a choice of CrewAI cloud, VPC, or your infrastructure.
