Runway

Video, image, and audio generation metered in credits.

Official siteCompareNo affiliateuntested

Runway generates video, images, and audio. The pricing page lists a free one-time credit grant and three paid plans. Yearly billing is 20% off the monthly price. Credits reset monthly except on Max, where one month can roll over.

Features and prices below are from the vendor site on the last-checked date. They are not ShipRank test results.

What the vendor lists

  • Credits pay for video, image, and audio. The pricing page gives per-model rates, such as Gen-4.5 video.
  • Standard and above list an agentic creative collaborator and App Studio.
  • Paid plans include music, voice, and sound effects generation.
  • Extra credits can be purchased. The FAQ says the minimum purchase is 1,000 credits.

Use cases

Generate a short video clip · Generate images

Pricing · checked 2026-11-08

Free is $0 with 125 credits, once. Standard is $15/month or $12/month yearly (625 credits/month). Pro is $35/month or $28/month yearly (2,250 credits/month). Max is $95/month or $76/month yearly (9,500 credits/month). The page says yearly billing saves 20%.

Free tier: yes

Free includes 125 credits one time, not a monthly refill, plus 5 GB storage and a selection of models. Paid plans remove watermarks.

  • Free — $0 · 125 credits, one time. 5 GB storage.
  • Standard — $15/month · Yearly $12/month. 625 credits/month. No watermark.
  • Pro — $35/month · Yearly $28/month. 2,250 credits/month.
  • Max — $95/month · Yearly $76/month. 9,500 credits/month. Up to one month of unused credits rolls over.
