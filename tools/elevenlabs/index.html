ElevenLabs

Voice, music, and dubbing, with an agents product line on the same pricing page.

Official siteCompareNo affiliateuntested

ElevenLabs sells synthetic voice and related audio tools, and the pricing page also has tabs for ElevenAgents and an API. Creative-plan prices below are the monthly figures in the pricing FAQ. A first-month promo was on the page the day it was checked.

Features and prices below are from the vendor site on the last-checked date. They are not ShipRank test results.

What the vendor lists

  • One credit pool covers text to speech, speech to text, music, sound effects, and dubbing, per the FAQ.
  • The pricing page has separate ElevenCreative, ElevenAgents, and ElevenAPI sections.
  • Instant voice cloning is listed from Starter. Professional voice cloning is listed from Creator.
  • The FAQ says unused paid credits can roll over for up to two months, up to a cap, and Free credits do not roll over.

Use cases

Generate voiceover · Run a voice agent · Dub video

Pricing · checked 2026-11-08

Monthly prices in the FAQ: Free $0 (10,000 credits), Starter $6 (30,000), Creator $22 (121,000), Pro $99 (600,000), Scale $299 (1,800,000, 3 seats), Business $990 (6,000,000, 10 seats). Enterprise is custom. The page also showed Starter at $1 and Creator at $11 for the first month. Annual billing is the monthly price times 10.

Free tier: yes
