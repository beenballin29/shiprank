HeyGen

Avatar video from a script, with a short free monthly allowance.

Official siteCompareNo affiliateuntested

HeyGen generates presenter-style video from a script, plus translation and a video agent. The pricing page and its FAQ agree on Free, Creator, Pro, Business, and Enterprise. Pro is a credit ladder, not one fixed bundle.

Features and prices below are from the vendor site on the last-checked date. They are not ShipRank test results.

What the vendor lists

  • Avatar video from a script, with stock presenters and a custom avatar.
  • Video Agent is listed on every paid tier, with usage that increases by plan.
  • Creator exports 1080p without the HeyGen watermark. Pro adds 4K export.
  • Credits are the meter. The FAQ says monthly unused credits roll over for one month.

Use cases

Generate an avatar video · Translate a video

Pricing · checked 2026-11-08

Free is $0. Creator is $29/month, and the FAQ says $24/month if you pay annually. Pro starts at $49/month. The FAQ says Pro tiers run from 1,000 credits at $49 to 100,000 credits at $4,300, with the same features. Business is $149/month plus $20 per extra seat. Enterprise is contact sales.

Free tier: yes

Free includes 3 watermarked videos a month, up to 1 minute each, plus limited Avatar IV and Video Agent access. No card required, per the FAQ.

  • Free — $0 · 3 videos/month, watermarked, up to 1 minute.
  • Creator — $29/month · FAQ: $24/month on annual billing. 600 credits. 1080p. Branding removed.
  • Pro — From $49/month · Same features across credit tiers. FAQ tops out at 100,000 credits for $4,300/month.
  • Business — $149/month · 1,500 credits on the card. Extra seats $20/month. SSO.
  • Enterprise — Contact sales
