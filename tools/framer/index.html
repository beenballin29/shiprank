Framer is a design tool that publishes sites. The pricing page, with yearly billing selected in the extract, showed a free plan, Basic, and Pro. AI features spend workspace credits. The MCP server was marked as a preview.

Features and prices below are from the vendor site on the last-checked date. They are not ShipRank test results.

What the vendor lists

  • Design and publish a site, with CMS collections on paid plans.
  • AI agents and other AI features consume credits. Free workspaces get a one-time 500-credit try, per the FAQ.
  • Paid site plans include a custom domain. Editors are an extra per-seat charge.
  • The pricing page lists an MCP server as a preview on Free, Basic, and Pro.

Use cases

Publish a landing page · Design a marketing site

Pricing · checked 2026-11-08

With yearly billing selected, the page showed Free at $0, Basic at $10/month, and Pro at $30/month. Enterprise is custom. Additional editors are $20/month. The page says prices are monthly figures billed on the cycle you pick at checkout, so the monthly-toggle amounts can differ.

Free tier: yes

Free includes a Framer domain, design pages, and 500 AI credits to try when the workspace has no paid subscription. A custom domain requires a paid plan.

  • Free — $0 · 500 AI credits to try. Framer domain. 1 GB bandwidth listed on the card.
  • Basic — $10/month on yearly billing · Custom domain. 1,000 AI credits/month in the agents row.
  • Pro — $30/month on yearly billing · 3,000 AI credits/month. Staging and redirects.
  • Enterprise — Custom · SSO, SCIM, uptime guarantee.
