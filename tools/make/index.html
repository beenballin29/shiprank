Make

Visual automation billed in credits per action.

Official siteCompareNo affiliateuntested

Make (formerly Integromat) runs scenarios that move data between apps. Since a pricing change the help center dates to 2025, the meter is credits, and most actions cost one credit. Entry prices below are from Make’s own comparison with Zapier. The live pricing page uses a credit slider, so the bill rises with volume.

Features and prices below are from the vendor site on the last-checked date. They are not ShipRank test results.

What the vendor lists

  • Scenarios connect apps. A run can spend anywhere from two credits to thousands, per the pricing FAQ.
  • The FAQ says router and error-handler modules do not consume credits. Most other actions do.
  • Modules that use Make’s own AI provider can cost more than one credit.
  • Extra credits can be bought, and Core, Pro, and Teams can auto-buy 10,000 credits.

Use cases

Automate a repeated task

Pricing · checked 2026-11-08

Make’s comparison page lists Free at $0 with 1,000 credits/month, Core from $9/month, Pro from $16/month, and Teams from $29/month. Each paid plan starts at 10,000 credits/month. Enterprise is custom. Higher credit volumes cost more than those starting prices.

Free tier: yes

Free includes 1,000 credits a month. The pricing FAQ says most actions cost 1 credit and that scenarios stop when credits run out.

  • Free — $0 · 1,000 credits/month.
  • Core — From $9/month · Starts at 10,000 credits/month. Price scales up with credits.
  • Pro — From $16/month · Starts at 10,000 credits/month.
  • Teams — From $29/month · Starts at 10,000 credits/month.
  • Enterprise — Custom
