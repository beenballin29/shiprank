Gumloop

Agents and workflows that call your other tools, billed in credits.

Official siteCompareNo affiliateuntested

Gumloop (AgentHub Inc.) runs agents and workflows against connected apps. The credit docs, not the mostly empty pricing-page extract, are the price source: Pro is $37/month. There is no standing free plan. A 14-day Pro trial requires a card.

Features and prices below are from the vendor site on the last-checked date. They are not ShipRank test results.

What the vendor lists

  • Agent chats spend credits on the model, tool calls, compute time, and an orchestration fee.
  • Docs price a successful connector call at 1 credit plus any tool-specific charge.
  • Bring-your-own-key model calls can drop the model portion of the bill to zero credits on Pro.
  • Cancelling the trial before it ends avoids the first charge, per the billing help page.

Use cases

Automate a repeated task · Run a multi-step agent

Pricing · checked 2026-11-08

Docs list Pro at $37/month with 20,000 credits, and Enterprise as custom. One credit is defined there as $0.005. Overage is billed at that rate and is capped. Credits do not roll over on Pro.

Free tier: no

No standing free plan in the credit docs. New accounts get a one-time 14-day Pro trial that requires a card and becomes a paid Pro subscription unless cancelled.

  • Pro — $37/month · 20,000 credits/month. Unused credits do not roll over.
  • Enterprise — Custom · Custom credits. Unused credits roll over. VPC option is mentioned on the product site.
